Shipping & returns

When will my order ship?+

Every shirt is printed to order, so give us 2 to 5 business days to make it, then 3 to 6 more for US delivery. Most US orders land within about a week to a week and a half. You get a tracking link by email the moment it ships.

How much is shipping?+

It is calculated at checkout based on your address and shown before you pay. No handling fees, no surprises.

Do you ship internationally?+

Yes. International delivery usually runs 10 to 20 business days after your order ships, depending on customs.

Can I return or exchange a shirt?+

If your order arrives damaged, misprinted, or wrong, we replace or refund it for free. Just email us within 30 days with a photo. Because each shirt is made on demand, we cannot take returns for the wrong size chosen or a change of mind, so check the size guide first, or email us and we will help you pick.

I need to cancel or fix my address.+

Email us within 2 hours of ordering and we will do our best to catch it before it prints.

Fit & fabric

What shirt do you print on?+

The Comfort Colors 1717: a 6.1 oz heavyweight, garment-dyed ring-spun cotton tee with a relaxed unisex fit. It starts soft and keeps its shape.

How does the sizing run?+

True to size with a relaxed fit. If you like extra room, size up. Every product page has a full size guide with chest and length measurements.

Why does the color look a little different than the photo?+

Garment-dyed cotton varies slightly batch to batch, and screens show color differently. Small variation in shade and print placement is normal and part of the character, not a defect.

How do I care for it?+

Machine wash cold, inside out, and tumble dry low. The garment dye softens and fades a touch with age on purpose. It only gets better.

Orders & the studio

Are these your own designs?+

Yes, every one. Muskrat & Rorke is a small Atlanta studio and each design is drawn in-house, start to finish. No trend scraping, no clip art.

Where is my order made?+

Printed to order in the United States, at the production partner nearest you, so it travels less to reach you.

How do I reach a human?+

Email support@muskratandrorke.com. We are small and a real person reads every message.

Still stuck?

We would rather answer a question than have you guess. Email us and we will sort it out.
