Muskrat & Rorke is a small studio in Atlanta making shirts about whatever won't leave us alone: cats versus gravity, the politics of personal space, the case for fixing wrongs instead of ignoring them.

Every design is ours, start to finish. No trend scraping, no clip art, no filler. If a sketch makes us grin or gush, it gets printed.
